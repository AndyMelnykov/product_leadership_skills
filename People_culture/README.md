# People & Culture

## Purpose

This skill area helps product leaders build healthy teams, create trust, and enable sustainable execution.

## Core skills

- team coaching and development
- feedback and accountability
- conflict resolution and healthy disagreement
- psychological safety and high-performance habits
- leadership communication and role clarity
- hiring, onboarding, and team capability building

## Practice questions

- What does the team need to do better together?
- Where is clarity missing or ownership unclear?
- What feedback would help the team improve safely and quickly?
- How do we create momentum without overload?

## Good habits

- coach before directing
- reward learning and transparency
- make commitments realistic and explicit
- support execution without micromanaging
- treat team health as a product metric

## Improvement loop

Review team interactions after milestones and decisions. Look for patterns in trust, clarity, conflict, and performance, and adapt your leadership style accordingly.
