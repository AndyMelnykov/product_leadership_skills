# Business Structure & Stakeholders

## Purpose

This skill area helps product leaders shape the operating model, align stakeholders, and make decisions that work across the organization.

## Core skills

- stakeholder mapping and influence
- executive communication and narrative framing
- negotiation and trade-off management
- cross-functional alignment and dependency management
- governance, decision rights, and escalation paths
- business case and prioritization communication

## Practice questions

- Who are the key stakeholders for this decision?
- What are their goals, constraints, and risks?
- Where are the incentives misaligned?
- What decision needs to be made, by whom, and by when?

## Good habits

- clarify decision owners early
- make trade-offs explicit
- communicate reason, not just outcome
- keep stakeholder updates concise and relevant
- convert ambiguity into decisions with assumptions documented

## Improvement loop

Use each stakeholder conversation as a learning opportunity: capture what changed, what objections appeared, and what would make the next alignment easier.
