# Strategy Thinking & Storytelling

## Purpose

This skill area helps product leaders connect short-term work to long-term direction and communicate it in a way that creates alignment and action.

## Core skills

- strategic thinking and pattern recognition
- framing the problem and the opportunity
- narrative design and messaging
- scenario planning and trade-off discussion
- decision quality and communication discipline
- building shared understanding across the organization

## Practice questions

- What is the strategic problem we are really solving?
- What assumptions are shaping this direction?
- Which narrative will create alignment and momentum?
- What are the trade-offs, and how are we communicating them?

## Good habits

- connect decisions to outcomes and customer value
- simplify complexity without losing nuance
- tell the story behind the numbers
- make strategic choices visible and explainable
- revisit assumptions as context changes

## Improvement loop

Review how your strategy and communication landed. Notice what created buy-in, what created confusion, and how to improve future framing and storytelling.
