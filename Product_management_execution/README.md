# Product Execution

## Purpose

This competency area focuses on the practical ability to turn product thinking into reliable delivery. It is one of the four foundations in the PM competency model developed by Ravi Mehta.

## Why this competency matters

Strong product execution helps teams move from ambiguity to clarity. It supports product leaders in creating better specs, aligning with delivery partners, and improving product quality over time.

## Core competencies

### Feature Specification
- gather requirements clearly
- define scope and intent in a way the team can act on
- create actionable specifications with measurable outcomes

### Product Delivery
- coordinate closely with engineering and design
- sequence work for fast, iterative learning
- manage dependencies and unblock execution

### Product Quality
- identify quality gaps early
- prioritize functional, technical, and business issues
- improve the product experience and business results over time

## How this connects to review and hiring

This competency should be evident in:

- performance reviews through examples of shipped clarity, delivery effectiveness, and quality outcomes
- manager coaching through feedback on spec quality and execution habits
- hiring through interview questions that test prioritization, communication, and delivery judgment

Interview questions should be aligned to the same criteria used in evaluation so candidates are assessed against the actual work of the role.

## Practice questions

- How clearly are requirements and expected outcomes defined?
- Where are the key dependencies or trade-offs not yet resolved?
- What quality risks could block user value?
- How are we learning and iterating as we deliver?

## Improvement loop

Review the quality of decisions, specs, and delivery outcomes after each major milestone. Capture what worked, where clarity was missing, and which habits improved execution over time.
