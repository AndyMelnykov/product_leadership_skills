# Product Leadership Skills

## Repository goal

This repository is a growing set of practical skills for product leaders who want to build better products, lead with clarity, and work effectively in modern teams. It is designed to help product leaders:

- build product strategy and execution that create real customer value
- work well with teams, stakeholders, and cross-functional partners
- collaborate effectively with AI agents as thought partners and execution assistants
- improve continuously by learning from real usage, feedback, and reflection

The purpose is not just to collect knowledge, but to create a reusable system for decision-making, collaboration, learning, and leadership over time.

## PM competency model

This repository includes a product management competency model based on Ravi Mehta’s PM competency model. The model is useful because it helps people grow and align expectations across three connected workflows:

- development and coaching
- performance review and feedback
- hiring and interview calibration

The core idea is simple: competencies define what good looks like, performance reviews assess evidence of those competencies, and interviews should be designed to evaluate the same capabilities using aligned questions and criteria.

When teams use the same competency language across development, evaluation, and hiring, people get clearer expectations, managers make more consistent decisions, and the organization builds a healthier product culture.

## What this skills set helps with

### 1. Build product

Product leadership requires strong product thinking and execution. This includes:

- identifying problems worth solving
- framing opportunities and priorities
- translating customer needs into product direction
- making trade-offs across value, feasibility, and business impact
- creating roadmaps, experiments, and delivery plans
- measuring outcomes and refining based on evidence

### 2. Work with the team and stakeholders

Strong product leaders align people around the right goals and decisions. This includes:

- communicating product vision and rationale clearly
- facilitating alignment across engineering, design, marketing, sales, and executives
- managing stakeholder expectations and competing priorities
- building trust, accountability, and healthy team dynamics
- resolving tension through structured conversations and decision-making
- creating momentum without creating chaos

### 3. Work with AI agents

AI is becoming a core collaborator for product leaders. This repository supports practical use of AI by emphasizing:

- using AI to speed up research, synthesis, planning, and documentation
- defining clear goals, constraints, and review checkpoints
- delegating repeatable tasks while keeping human judgment in charge
- validating AI output against evidence, business context, and user needs
- creating a reliable workflow where AI enhances decision quality instead of replacing it

### 4. Self-improve based on skills usage

The most effective skill systems evolve with practice. This repository is built around a learning loop:

- reflect on how and when a skill was used
- assess what worked, what did not, and why
- capture examples, patterns, and lessons learned
- improve prompts, frameworks, and habits over time
- turn repeated experience into clearer judgment and better execution

## Core skill areas

This repository organizes learning across these areas:

- Product strategy and discovery
- Prioritization and decision-making
- Roadmapping and execution
- Stakeholder communication and influence
- Team leadership and collaboration
- AI-assisted product workflows
- Learning, reflection, and continuous improvement
- PM competency model application in reviews, growth, and hiring

## Competency support skills

The repo includes a Claude Code skills folder with skills that map directly to the competency model: `synthesize-research`, `user-research`, `write-feature-spec`, `stakeholder-alignment-brief`, `prep-competency-review`, `build-dashboard`, and `data-visualization`. Each one ties its output back to a specific sub-competency so it can double as evidence in coaching, review, or hiring conversations.

## How to use this repository

Use this repository as a practical playbook for product leadership:

- study the skills and frameworks
- apply them in real product decisions and team situations
- log what worked and what should change
- refine your process through reflection and iteration
- build a personal system for product leadership that improves over time

## Intended outcome

The goal is to help product leaders become more strategic, more effective in execution, more trusted in communication, and more capable in an AI-enabled environment. In short, this repo is meant to support better product decisions, stronger teams, and continuous growth.
