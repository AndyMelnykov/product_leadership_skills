# Product Management

## Purpose

This skill area focuses on turning customer need, business opportunity, and technical feasibility into product decisions that create measurable value.

## Core skills

- problem discovery and opportunity framing
- product strategy and roadmap thinking
- prioritization and trade-off decisions
- experiment design and validation
- metrics and outcome measurement
- execution coordination with design and engineering

## Practice questions

- What problem are we solving, and for whom?
- Why is this the right time to act?
- What evidence proves that the opportunity is real?
- What is the smallest meaningful step toward learning?
- How will we know this created value?

## Good habits

- define the user problem before the solution
- use evidence to challenge assumptions
- prioritize based on value, urgency, and confidence
- keep roadmaps directional, not overly rigid
- review outcomes regularly and refine quickly

## Improvement loop

Track product decisions and outcomes over time. Learn which assumptions were validated, which bets failed, and which decision patterns repeatedly created clarity and impact.
